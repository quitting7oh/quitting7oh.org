[/u/FlyAdventurous6231](https://www.reddit.com/user/FlyAdventurous6231/)
spent close to two years doing the work that made this site possible. He
gathered scattered reports and compared them with what community members were
seeing. He turned that material into practical guides and kept answering urgent
questions as new people arrived.

## The work behind these pages

Most of the original information behind quitting7oh.org grew from what Fly
collected and shared. He assembled the most complete practical body of
information this community had on quitting 7-OH, then kept revising it as the
products and the community's experience changed.

The protocols and explanations on these pages owe much to that work. This site
gives them a permanent, searchable home.

## The time he gave

Fly spent nights, days, and weekends supporting people through withdrawal and
helping them find a next step. For many people, he was the person they looked to
when the path forward was hard to see.

quitting7oh.org is dedicated to Fly and the knowledge he shared with this
community.

**Thank you, Fly.**
